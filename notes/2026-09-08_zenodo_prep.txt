10. WILLOTT NORMALIZATION, CHECKED, NOTHING CHANGED
jet_power.py does divide by 4pi. It computes L3 as the total luminosity in W/Hz, scales it
to 151 MHz, then has L151_sr = L151 / (4*np.pi) on its own line before that goes into
Q = 3e38 * (L151_sr/1e28)**(6/7). So the Willott input is per steradian, as it should be.
Medians with the division are 35.11 and 35.76, which is what the paper prints. Without it
they would be 36.05 and 36.70. The difference is log10(4pi)*6/7 = 0.9422, the -0.94 dex
expected. Reported to Valeria, code left alone.
