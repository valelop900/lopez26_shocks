OIII census with doublet-tied fit, 2026-07-13

Improved the OIII fit: 4959 and 5007 now fit simultaneously with tied parameters
(same velocity center and width, 4959 amplitude fixed to 1/3 of 5007, atomic physics).
Same approach as Mullaney et al fits used by Molyneux. Window covers both lines,
baseline excludes the whole doublet.

Census, broad detected = broad amp > 3x its own fit error:
large: 2 of 16 no broad (13%)
small: 17 of 51 no broad (33%)
1 object excluded for unusable error: 39627701220278777

Result stable across all three methods tried (amp cutoff, error-based single-line,
error-based doublet): no-broad objects are 2-3x more common in the small sample.

Direction is opposite the Molyneux compact-source trend, but our samples (10-15 vs
>20 kpc) sit entirely in their "extended" regime (their compact = <5 kpc), so this
probes a different size range than their result.
