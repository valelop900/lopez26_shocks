TWO PROBLEMS FOUND - NOT FIXED, NEED MARK
1. Jet power spectral index. There are two different calculations in the repo and the paper
   uses both in one sentence. plot_loi_vs_q.py (Figure 4) converts 3 GHz to 151 MHz with a
   FLAT alpha = -0.7, which is what the Discussion says. jet_power.py uses each source's own
   Alpha with -0.7 only as a fallback. The medians quoted in that same sentence, 35.5 and
   35.6, are the per-source ones. Flat gives 35.11 small and 35.76 large. Figure 4's x-axis
   limits (41.6 to 44.6 in erg/s) confirm the figure is the flat version; per-source would
   start at 40.7. A factor of ~4.5 between the samples would break "nearly identical median
   jet powers" in the Conclusions. Decision 9 in the README, raised in the license email.
2. The released flux columns do not reproduce the released bpt_class / sl_distance.
   notes/2026-07-31_bpt_class_lumcut_v2.csv has log_oi_ha -1.77 to 0.30 and log_oiii_hb
   -0.35 to 1.66, which is what Figure 1 plots. Recomputing those from the shipped agngal
   fluxes gives -1.72 to -0.18 and -0.53 to 1.55: median offset 0.14 dex in x, 0.18 in y,
   worst case 1.00 and 1.18. sl_distance then only reproduces to about 0.12 median, 0.67
   worst. Same story for Figure 4's y-axis, which uses the pyspeckit [OI] flux, not
   OI_6300_FLUX (ratio between the two runs 0.12 to 1.90, median 1.07). So Figures 2, 3 and
   5 regenerate from the release and Figures 1 and 4 do not.
   Added INCLUDE_FIT_RATIOS to zenodo/make_release_tables.py, which merges log_oi_ha and
   log_oiii_hb in. Tested: with it on, sl_distance reproduces from the columns to 1e-4.
   Left at False pending Mark. Documented as decision 11 and in the column notes.
   The Balmer decrement and the [NeV] fractions DO come from the shipped columns and
   reproduce exactly, so this is specific to the BPT ratios and the [OI] luminosity.
