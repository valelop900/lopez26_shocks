No-broad-component fraction by sample, 2026-07-09

Question from Mark: is a missing broad OIII component more or less common in the large vs small sample.
Mark's reaction: he would have guessed the opposite (more broad in small sources), so result was verified.

Using notes/2026-07-08_oiii_fits.csv, no broad = broad amplitude < 1.

large sample: 3 of 16 no broad (19%)
small sample: 18 of 52 no broad (35%)

Verified with scripts/verify_broad_fraction.py:
- membership: 16 large, 52 small, 0 overlap, 0 unmatched
- cutoff sensitivity: large stays 3/16 at cutoffs 0.5, 1.0, 2.0. small moves 13, 18, 21.
  So the small-sample fraction is cutoff-dependent but the direction (small has more
  no-broad objects than large) holds at every cutoff.
