This is the measurement table behind Lopez and Lacy, "Evidence for strong shocks in the ISM of small radio sources", submitted to The Astrophysical Journal Letters. Two samples of radio-loud active galactic nuclei were drawn from a cross-match of the VLA Sky Survey with Dark Energy Spectroscopic Instrument Data Release 1 spectroscopy, over redshift 0.15 to 0.5, with [O I] 6300 detected above five sigma, and cut to a rest-frame 3 GHz luminosity above 10^24 W per Hz. The small sample of 47 sources has jets of 10 to 15 kiloparsecs still confined within the host galaxy; the large sample of 30 sources has jets larger than 25 kiloparsecs that have broken out. Both are given in one table of 77 rows keyed by DESI TARGETID, with a sample column separating them.

For every source the table gives the VLASS radio properties, the emission line fluxes and inverse variances from the DESI DR1 agngal value-added catalog, the paper's own pyspeckit line fluxes and the two BPT ratios derived from them, the [O III] 5007 W80 line width with the instrumental resolution removed, and the BPT classification with its distance from the Seyfert-LINER dividing line. Also included are a machine-readable column description, the scripts that build the table and make the figures, and a check script that reproduces every statistic in the paper from the released table alone. The DESI spectra and the VLASS images are not redistributed here; they are public and can be retrieved using the TARGETID column.
