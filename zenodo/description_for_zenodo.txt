Measurement table behind Lopez and Lacy, "Evidence for strong shocks in the ISM of small radio sources", submitted to The Astrophysical Journal Letters. We matched the VLA Sky Survey to Dark Energy Spectroscopic Instrument Data Release 1 spectra and kept radio-loud active galactic nuclei at redshift 0.15 to 0.5 with [O I] 6300 detected above five sigma and a rest-frame 3 GHz luminosity above 10^24 W per Hz. The small sample of 44 sources has jets of 10 to 15 kiloparsecs, still inside the host galaxy. The large sample of 33 sources has jets larger than 25 kiloparsecs that have broken out. We give both in one table of 77 rows keyed by DESI TARGETID, with a sample column.

For each source the table gives the VLASS radio properties, the line fluxes and inverse variances from the DESI DR1 agngal value-added catalog, our pyspeckit line fluxes with the two BPT ratios, the [O III] 5007 W80 width with the instrumental resolution removed, the BPT class with its distance from the Seyfert-LINER line, and the host morphology from the optical images. We also include a column description file, the scripts that build the table and make the figures, and a check script that gives every number in the paper from the table alone. We do not include the DESI spectra or the VLASS images. They are public and the TARGETID column finds them.
